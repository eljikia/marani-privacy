MARANI

Community Guidelines & Terms

Last updated November 12, 2026

Marani is a wine social club for collectors, curious drinkers and friends. These guidelines keep it a warm, welcoming place. By creating a profile or using Marani’s social features, you agree to them.

Be someone people want to share a bottle with

  • Be kind. Disagree about wine all you like; don’t attack people.
  • Keep it about wine, food and the good company around them.
  • Be yourself. Don’t impersonate other people or brands, and don’t use a name or handle that’s offensive or misleading.

Not allowed on Marani

There is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users. You may not post, share or use Marani for:

  • Harassment, bullying, threats, or hate based on race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability or any other identity.
  • Sexual, violent, or otherwise offensive content.
  • Spam, scams, fake accounts, or misleading events.
  • Selling or delivering alcohol, or arranging sales between members. Marani is for sharing what you enjoy, not a marketplace.
  • Encouraging underage or irresponsible drinking, or drunk driving.
  • Sharing someone else’s private information, including home addresses, without their permission.
  • Anything illegal where you or other members live.

Events

  • Only post real events you’re actually hosting, with honest details.
  • Keep exact home addresses out of public event details. Share them with guests directly.
  • Events are for adults of legal drinking age. Hosts are responsible for their own gatherings; Marani doesn’t organise, supervise or vet events.

Reporting and blocking

  • Report a member or an event from the ••• menu on their page. Reports are private.
  • Block anyone, any time. They won’t be able to follow you or see your shelf, and you’ll stop following each other.
  • We review reports within 24 hours. If something breaks these guidelines, we remove it, and we may suspend or permanently remove the account responsible.

Your account

  • You must be of legal drinking age where you live, and at least 18.
  • You’re responsible for what you share and for keeping your sign-in secure.
  • You keep ownership of what you post. You give Marani permission to store and show it to the people your settings allow, only to run the app.
  • You can delete your account at any time in the app (Vineyard → Delete account). See the Privacy Policy for what that removes.
  • We may remove content or accounts that break these guidelines, and we may change or stop features of the app.

The fine print

Marani is provided as is, without warranties. To the extent the law allows, Marani isn’t liable for content posted by members or for events they host. Please drink responsibly. These terms work alongside Apple’s standard end-user licence agreement.
