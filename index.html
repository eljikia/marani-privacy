Marani

Privacy Policy · Last updated November 8, 2026

Marani is a personal wine-cellar app. It has no accounts, no servers of its own, and no analytics or advertising. I do not collect, store, or sell any of your data.

What stays on your phone

Your bottles, notes and photos are stored only on your device. Marani does not upload them anywhere. If you delete the app, that data is deleted with it.

Reading wine labels

By default, labels are read on your device using Apple’s text recognition and, on supported iPhones, Apple’s on-device language model. The photo never leaves your phone.

Optionally, you can add your own Anthropic API key in Settings and choose “Claude” as the label reader. If you do, the label photo you scan is sent directly from your phone to Anthropic (api.anthropic.com) using your key, and is handled under Anthropic’s terms and privacy policy. I never see it.

Third-party services the app contacts

  • Apple (geocoding): to place a wine’s region on the Map tab, the region name (for example “Vosne-Romanée”) is sent to Apple’s geocoding service.
  • Map tiles: the Map tab loads map tiles from Esri and the Leaflet library from unpkg.com. Like any web request, this exposes your IP address to those providers. No wine data or personal information is sent.

Camera and photos

Marani asks for camera or photo access only so you can photograph or choose a label picture. Photos you choose are used inside the app only.

Children

Marani is about wine and is not directed at children.
